Eligibility AI Expanded Access is available as an add-on to Google Workspace customers with the following editions: Business Standard Business Plus Enterprise Standard Enterprise Plus AI Expanded Access is available in all countries where Google Workspace is available . Don't assign AI Expanded Access licenses to users under age 18. Learn more about language support in Google Workspace with Gemini.
